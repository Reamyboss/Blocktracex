## Why Blocktracex Exists

Modern software engineering produces enormous amounts of information but very little understanding.

Developers know how many files exist.

They know how many tests pass.

They know which framework they're using.

What they often don't know is:

• How healthy is this project?
• How mature is our engineering?
• Where are the biggest risks?
• Is our architecture improving?
• Are we accumulating technical debt?
• What should we improve next?

Good tools inspect projects.

Great tools help engineers understand them.

Blocktracex exists to bridge that gap.

## Vision

Our vision is to become the Engineering Intelligence Platform developers trust to understand software systems.

We believe software projects should be able to explain themselves through evidence, engineering knowledge, and intelligent reasoning.

Our long-term mission is to evolve Blocktracex from project inspection into the global standard for engineering intelligence.
